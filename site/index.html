Release notesInstall and remove TinyDash

Your choices, on your computer.

Choose whether to save clipboard history. Choose which folders to search. Your settings and saved history stay in your user account.

Web searches open your browser. Currency rates use an internet service. You can turn rate updates off in Settings.

Removing the app keeps your settings and history. The removal guide explains how to delete them, including recovery backups.
